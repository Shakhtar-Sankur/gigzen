Why Waggle

A neighbourhood shop, the person ordering from it, and the rider carrying the order each see only their own piece of a delivery. Waggle puts all three on one network: every step is shared live, checked with a code, and paid in the open.

Waggle

For customers. Order from nearby shops, alone or as a group, now or later, and follow the rider to your door. Or send a parcel with Waggle Send.

Waggle Business

For shops. Take orders, turn each one into a delivery in a tap, watch the rider on a map, and keep GST invoices and payments in one place.

Waggle Gig

For riders. Offers nearby with the fare up front, spoken directions to the shop and the door, and earnings by the hour. The whole fare is yours.

Waggle Send

Parcels across town: documents, keys, a lunch left at home. Bike-size, up to 10 kg. You see the price first and pay the rider directly.

Checked with our own tests: 150 attacks on the database rules, all blocked, and 42 steps clicked through all three apps live, from a new rider's verification to the rating at the end of a delivery.

Get the apps

  1. 01

    Everyone is verified

    Riders send an ID, a selfie and, on a motorbike, their driving licence and RC. Shops send PAN, their licences and a photo of the shop. A person at Gigzen checks each one before the first delivery.

  2. 02

    A code at both ends

    The shop gives the rider a pickup code; the customer gives a delivery code only at the door. A parcel is photographed when it's picked up.

  3. 03

    Only what each person needs

    A rider sees the full address and phone numbers only while carrying the order. A shop sees only its own riders on its map, and only while they carry its orders.

  4. 04

    Pay that's out in the open

    The rider keeps the whole fare. Shops pay riders straight to their UPI and record it, and a rider can flag a payment that never arrived.

These install from outside Google Play. Android will warn you first, and that's correct. Only continue if you downloaded the file from this page. If you tried an earlier test build of Waggle or Waggle Business, uninstall it first.
  1. Download the file on your Android phone.
  2. Open it. Android asks whether your browser may install apps. Allow it for the browser only.
  3. Install, then turn that permission off again. The apps don't need it.

All three are signed with Gigzen's release key. If you got a file anywhere else, check its SHA-256 matches before installing. The apps ask for your location, camera or photos only when a feature needs them.
