Android app

buzz buzz

Every kilometre counts. Track what you drive, see what you actually earned, and find the other drivers working your city.

built from how drivers really work.

01

Paid by distance, not by time

Earnings accrue from kilometres actually travelled. Sitting in traffic doesn't inflate the number — a filter throws away GPS drift under 15 metres.

02

A map with people on it

See friends moving in real time and search any city or shop worldwide. Sharing your position is opt-in, and stops the moment you turn it off.

03

Speaks your language

Sixteen languages including right-to-left Arabic, with the currency set automatically from where you are.

04

Messaging that behaves

Direct messages with delivery and read receipts, presence and last-seen. Messages send instantly rather than waiting on a poll.

05

Your data stays yours

Location is collected only while tracking is on — never in the background, never with the app closed. Delete your account and everything goes with it.

06

Goals worth chasing

Daily income targets, weekly distance challenges and a leaderboard among the drivers you know, resetting on a real schedule.

Status

In internal testing on Google Play. The signed release is live on the internal track. Closed testing comes next, then production. Not yet publicly downloadable.

Developer tool

populace

Fill your app with people before it has any. A simulated population uses your real API as real authenticated users — then reports everything that broke.

some bugs need more than one person.

You cannot test presence, read receipts or live sync while being one human with one account.

01

One adapter to integrate

Thirteen small methods, two required. Each answers one question: how does this happen in my app? Anything you skip is reported as untested, never as passing.

02

Through the front door

Every action uses the same API your users hit — no admin keys, no direct database writes. Your permission rules get tested by genuinely separate identities.

03

Evidence, not vibes

Latency percentiles per endpoint under concurrent load, failures grouped by shape, and a non-zero exit code so CI catches what you'd miss.

04

It refuses production

Three independent refusals. Your production hosts are denied however deeply nested in the config. No flag overrides them.
